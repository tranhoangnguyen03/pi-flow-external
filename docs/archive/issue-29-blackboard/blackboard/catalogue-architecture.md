# Catalogue — current proposed contract

Source: Catalogue session 01a07623-2100-73e8-8ca7-5e3ae8d9ca18, JSONL line 437 (write of ontology-contract-v0.2.md), updated line 484. These are proposed design contracts, not an agreed implementation or measured benchmark.

- Initial domain is US private credit. Framework and jurisdiction assumptions stay explicit and extensible; US does not imply every borrower uses US GAAP.
- Source files, logical evidence cards, coverage/context, scoped relationships, expectations and unresolved issues form the small common model. The initial seven-column manifest is insufficient as the complete contract.
- Question interpretation and facet translation remain model reasoning. Deterministic filtering only narrows candidates; unresolved candidates remain discoverable.
- A later amendment does not automatically obsolete its base. Return base plus scoped changes and effective-event evidence. Replacement can apply to a schedule, provision, calculation or period.
- Preserve package period separately from content coverage, including YTD and trailing windows. Effective date, issue date, discovery time and saved-run cutoff are distinct facts.
- Reported EBITDA is not a universally standardized measure. Reporting and contractual measurement bases can differ. Existing covenant extraction/document-set owners retain authority over definitions and obligations; catalogue routes to them rather than creating a competing authority.
- Expand investigation for missing support, qualifications, conflicts, applicability or insufficient granularity—even with a nonempty shelf. Log why; fallback rate alone is not correctness.
- Saved monitoring results remain immutable records of that run's conclusions. Label later source investigation separately.
- Human corrections are durable provenance-bearing overrides bound to source/hash. Changed content requires reconsideration. Extractor/profile/dependency changes may invalidate derived cards without changed source bytes.
- Local presence does not establish timely delivery. Blank certificate templates are not submissions. Preserve internal-vs-filename conflicts rather than inventing applicability.
- No universal metric ledger, chart of accounts or legal reasoning engine. Machine serialization and operational details remain open.

Line 484 update: progressive discovery is hierarchical entry plus lateral evidence links and scoped search/recovery. Facet lookup is one operation, not the whole interaction model.
